## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored in the surface, generic in the structure. 8/10 for brand, 4/10 for structure.

Uncopyable: the field and its brim, five drawn mood faces, two type axes, the four state colours as page tabs, and the copy — *"the pile keeps. what you did not get to is not waiting for you — it is just still there, which is a different thing."*

Stock: a 720px centred column, a lid with three icons, a hamburger over three destinations, cards in a stack. And the product's genuinely novel ideas — one question per note, "stopping is a place", "Squirrel chooses" — are not expressed in layout at all. The gate is a `<details>`; stopping is a link in a footer.

The consequence: the two screens that fell out of the frame (`held`, `moods`) do not read as this product, because subtract the surface and there is nothing underneath holding the shape.

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs` exit 0, zero findings. B control-tested the detector to prove it was not silently broken, then re-ran with `--no-config`: four `overused-font` hits on `"Inter"`, all waived deliberately in `.impeccable/config.json` for the wordmark. No Go-template false positives.

## Overall Impression

The visual world is finished and should be left alone. The structure has not caught up and is now the ceiling. Two real bugs sit underneath the design questions, and one of them silently moves data.

## What's Working

1. **Capture.** First element on home, one field, one button, posts without moving the page, writes the outcome inside the box you just used, holds your words when the write fails. Two steps, no navigation, authored failure states.
2. **The refusal of counts is structural, not disciplinary.** `itemsWhere()` reads `limit+1` rows so "is there more" needs no `count(*)` — no code path has a total to leak. `/enough` mounts with no store so it cannot start keeping score.
3. **Two voices, correctly assigned.** Machine axis for what the system owns, casual for his own words. Drawn faces, not emoji. The mascot in the empty states rather than a shrug icon.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Search returns tasks, calls them pile notes, and offers a verb that moves them
`SearchItems` (`notes.go:255`) has no `kind` clause; `toView()` carries no kind; `results.html` branches on state alone. Verified live: "ring the vet about the booster" is a task on `/tasks`, and searching `vet` renders it `IN THE PILE` with DONE / KEEP / DROP. Pressing KEEP sets `kept` and the task silently leaves the tasks screen. **Fix:** carry `Kind` into `resultView`; render `DECIDED {{.When}}` and the tasks verbs for a task; add `A TASK` to the open-note branch so the two views of one note finally agree.

### [P0] `/moods` is unreachable on any day the picker has something to offer
`href="/moods"` appears exactly once in the codebase (`home.html:204`), inside the `{{else}}` of `{{with .Offer}}`. So do `?ask=1` and `show me something anyway`. Verified live: home currently renders **zero** links to `/moods` or `?ask=1`. PRODUCT §7 promises "Home shows today's answer and a link". A mis-tapped *wiped* then suppresses two picker rules all day with no visible cause and no correction. **Fix:** move the answered mood out of the `{{else}}` into the offer's lead row, carrying `say something else` and `what you said before`.

### [P1] Two screens are outside the frame the last release existed to establish
`.every` has no width rule. Measured at 1280px: shoebox/homebox/chorebox/results all 720px at x=280; `/held` 440.7px at x=419.7; `/moods` 227.1px at x=526.4. The two are not even consistent with each other, because the width is content-driven. On held, the `<h2>` group headings out-shout the `<h1>` above them. **Fix:** `.every { width: min(720px, 100%) }`; demote held's group headings to the meta role.

### [P1] The frame's own rule is broken on four screens
`/tasks/done` and `/pile?q=bins` render **two** `<h1>`s; empty `/kept`, `/home`, `/buddy` and a no-match search render **zero**. The binding constraint was "one heading element, one class, on every screen that is a screen". **Fix:** hoist the h1 and views nav above every `{{if}}`; demote empty-state headings to a styled `<p>`.

### [P1] `.view` — the only navigation between a screen and its sub-views — is 20.9px tall
`pile.css:2255` is a bare inline anchor: no padding, no `min-height`, no media query. 47% of the 44px minimum, and it is how you reach `kept`, `archive` and `held`. Every other control measured is 44px or more. **Fix:** give it the same 44px target as everything else.

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (one thumb, interrupted):** two taps per note, and the gate re-shuts on every card — an interruption between the gate and the verb loses the decision with nothing written. `STOP ASKING` is thumb-adjacent to `DID IT`. Search cannot be cleared without a keyboard; there is no `×`. The search panel opens at x=4 on a 390px screen, off-margin and not visually attached to the icon that summoned it.

**Sam (keyboard, screen reader, 200%):** six of twelve `<summary>` elements carry `role="button"`, which overrides native disclosure semantics — and `aria-expanded` appears nowhere in the codebase, so those six announce no state at all. `aria-live="polite"` sits on a search-results `<h1>`, so every keystroke re-announces the page title. Two `<h1>`s on some screens, none on others. Held's `<h2>` is visually larger than its `<h1>`. Contrast, though, is clean: every new lid colour measures 4.7:1 or better.

**Ronald on a bad day (23:00):** `you decided this today` is accurate and is an accusation. A mis-tapped mood cannot be corrected or seen. The pile answers him with a question, and the gate hides the four answers so he cannot see that they are easy. `/enough` — the screen whose whole job is making stopping feel normal — renders `HOME` and `ACTUALLY, ONE MORE` as two identical pills.

## Questions to Consider

1. The lid menu ships the same three items as home's doors, omits home, and omits every leaf. If the menu is right, why does home have doors?
2. `/moods` costs a route, a template, a handler and a store method, and can be reached only on a day Squirrel has nothing to offer. What is the ratio of times it has been opened to times it has been built?
3. The gate hides four answers behind a question. On the day capacity is lowest, is being asked cheaper than being shown four words — and what evidence exists either way, given it was reasoned rather than observed?
4. The picker reads six rules and hands you one thing. The doors, the menu and the tabs all organise by row-kind — the organisation PRODUCT says the picker was built to replace. Which one is the product now?
5. `pile.js` carries a comment asserting one vocabulary across both capture paths, and they differ. How many other comments here describe an intention rather than the code beneath them?
6. Does home need three doors, or does it need the offer, the slot, and one way into everything else?
