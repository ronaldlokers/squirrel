One frame

Thirteen screens that were each designed well and never designed together. Every frame below is an iframe holding the real app — real templates, real stylesheet — with one delta file on top. They are iframes so each is a real viewport and the media queries mean what they say. Nothing here is a mockup.

button
a filled shape. Something changes.
link
underlined. You go somewhere else.
disclosure
a chevron that turns. Something opens here; nothing changes.

All three were underlined text in places. On the card, LATER went to another note and fix the words opened a box in place, and they looked identical. Disclosures had four appearances between them — a plain line, a filled pill, a plus sign, a quiet caption.

The frames scroll. Open the disclosures — the chevron is the whole of the second idea.
