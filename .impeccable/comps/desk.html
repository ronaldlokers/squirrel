What the chat feel was doing, and what does it now

Nothing in the product's rules changed. Every job the conversation was carrying has somewhere else to be.

wasA note arrives as a turn, and you scroll back to find it again.
nowA card that stays in the column its room owns, in the order it arrived.
wasOne composer at the bottom, and a row of chips deciding where the words go.
nowA slot at the top of each column. What you type lands in the room you are looking at.
wasBuddy speaks in bubbles, with a face and a time, in a column of his own.

The slot travels

On the desk each column has its own slot and there are four of them at once. Here there is one, directly under the tabs, and it says what this room does with words: what is it in the notes, what comes back? in the chores.

The offer stays on top of the tabs

It belongs to no room. On a phone you are usually here for one thing, so the thing Squirrel picked is above the choosing.

Today is a bar, not a screen

One line: what just happened, its undo, and the five faces. It empties overnight, so it can never grow into a list you are behind on.

What this costs

Capture is now two presses on a phone — pick the room, then type — where the dock was one. That is the price of never asking "where did this go", and it is the trade to argue with if the sofa scene starts to suffer.
